Quick Reference
Key rules from Sunset Aviation Op Specs and FAR — Certificate No. 7SSA382M

Part 91 — Destination Alternate (§91.169)

▶
ℹ1-2-3 Rule (§91.169(b))
An alternate is required unless, for the period from 1 hour before to 1 hour after ETA, the destination airport forecast weather is:

• Ceiling ≥ 2,000 ft AGL, AND
• Visibility ≥ 3 statute miles

If both conditions are met for the entire ±1hr window → No alternate required.
If either condition fails at any point in the window → Alternate required.
📋Airport Without IAP (§91.169(a)(2))
If no IAP has been published for the destination, an alternate airport with an IAP must always be filed.
📋Part 91 Alternate Minimums (§91.169(c))
Precision approach at alternate: 600 ft ceiling / 2 sm visibility
Non-precision approach at alternate: 800 ft ceiling / 2 sm visibility
No IAP at alternate: Ceiling and vis allowing descent from MEA, approach, and landing under basic VFR

Part 135 — Destination Alternate (§135.223)

▶
ℹP135 Alternate Required When (§135.223(a))
An alternate is required unless the destination airport has:

• A weather reporting facility, AND
• For the period from 1 hour before to 1 hour after ETA, the forecast ceiling is ≥ 1,500 ft AGL AND visibility ≥ 3 sm

Also required if the destination airport has no instrument approach procedure.
📋P135 Alternate Minimums (§135.221 + C055)
1 nav facility (NPA, CAT I, or circling): Approach MDA/DA(H) + 400 ft ceiling / + 1 sm visibility
2+ nav facilities (different runways): Higher approach + 200 ft / + ½ sm
GPS-based IAP: Subject to C055 b(8) requirements — aircraft in Table 2, C052 held, RAIM checked

Part 135 — Departure Alternate (§135.217)

▶
ℹDeparture Alternate Required When
Under §135.217, if the departure airport weather is below the CAT I landing minimums for the aircraft and approach at the departure airport, a departure alternate must be filed within 1 hour flight time (in still air, normal cruising speed, one engine inoperative for multi-engine).

The departure alternate must meet the alternate minimums of C055 at the estimated time of arrival at the alternate.

⚠Turbojet Restriction (C054 b)
A turbojet PIC must NOT conduct an IAP when destination visibility is reported < ¾ sm or RVR 4000 unless the pilot is qualified for Lower Landing Minimums (LLM). If destination visibility forecast < ¾ sm or RVR 4000: runway field length must be pre-determined at ≥115% of required, and precision instrument runway markings or RCL lights must be operative.

C052 — Reduced Precision CAT I (1800 RVR)

▶
Conditions for 1800 RVR
• FD, AP, or HUD approved for CAT I must be engaged to DA or IAM
• If FD/AP/HUD malfunctions below FAF, execute missed approach
• Flightcrew must demonstrate proficiency in ILS/GLS/RNAV(GPS) with LPV DA/HAT < 250 ft on FD/AP/HUD
• Part 97 SIAP must have 1800 RVR minimum
• Runway must have no TDZ lights / RCL lights (with inoperative exception conditions per C052)
