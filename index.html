Quick Reference
Key rules from Sunset Aviation Op Specs and FAR — Certificate No. 7SSA382M

Part 91 — §91.169 (1-2-3 Rule)

▶
ℹAlternate Required Unless — 1-2-3 Rule
For the period 1 hour before to 1 hour after ETA, the destination forecast must show:
• Ceiling ≥ 2,000 ft AGL, AND
• Visibility ≥ 3 statute miles

If either fails at any point → alternate required.

Alternate minimums (§91.169(c)):
• Precision approach at alternate: 600 ft / 2 sm
• Non-precision at alternate: 800 ft / 2 sm
• No IAP at alternate: ceiling/vis allowing VFR descent and landing

Part 135 — §135.223 / §135.221 / C055

▶
ℹAlternate Required When (§135.223)
An alternate is required unless the destination has a Part 97 IAP and for the period ±1 hr of ETA:

Ceiling threshold — §135.223(b)(1)/(2):
 • If circling approach authorized: ceiling ≥ 1,500 ft above lowest circling MDA
 • If no circling authorized: ceiling ≥ higher of (1,500 ft above lowest published minimum) or (2,000 ft above airport elevation)

Visibility threshold — §135.223(b)(3):
 • Visibility ≥ higher of 3 sm or (planned approach vis minimum + 2 sm)

Both thresholds must be met throughout the entire ±1 hr ETA window. Conditional TAF clauses (TEMPO, PROB) count if more restrictive.
Dep. Alternate — §135.217
Required when departure weather is below CAT I landing minimums. Alternate must be within 1 hour OEI flight time and meet C055 Table 1 minimums at ETA.
